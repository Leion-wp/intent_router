# Leion Delivery Founder Sales

## Current offer

Sell `Leion Delivery Founding Pilot`, not the full Leion ecosystem and not a generic AI seat.

## ICP priority

1. software agencies and factories with repeated GitHub delivery work
2. CTOs of small product teams shipping fast with a thin engineering process
3. platform or dev productivity owners who want controlled AI workflows

## 30-day founder targets

- `100` targeted outbound contacts
- `10` qualified discovery calls
- `4` live demos
- `2` pilot proposals

## Qualification signals

- active GitHub-based workflow
- recurring issue to PR or review bottlenecks
- visible need for approval or governance
- enough engineering maturity to pilot on one repo
- budget authority for a `4 500 EUR` pilot or a clear path to it

## Disqualifiers

- no GitHub-centered delivery process
- pure marketing or non-technical buyer for this offer
- desire for full autonomy with no review points
- no owner available for a pilot

## Discovery questions

- How do issues become code changes today?
- Where does review slow you down?
- What release checks are still manual?
- Where would AI help, and where do you need hard approval?
- How many repos or teams would matter first?
- Who owns the workflow change if a pilot works?

## Outreach angles

- reduce repetitive delivery work without removing review
- standardize PR and release flows with clear approval points
- move from ad hoc prompting to reusable governed workflows
- keep execution local-first while adding a control plane over time
